Department Mission

Our goal is to ensure the safety and fluidity of all Liberty County roadways. From Springfield to the Industrial Park, we maintain the infrastructure that keeps our citizens moving.

Patrol Guidelines

Units must be in full uniform and operating a marked DOT vehicle. All road closures must be marked with proper cone placement and high-visibility lighting.

Radio Discipline

Keep comms clear of chatter. Use "10-8" when starting your shift at the DOT HQ (Postal 304). Report all debris and major accidents immediately.

Authorized Equipment

Standard issue includes reflective vests, road flares, and traffic cones. Heavy equipment (Flatbeds) are reserved for Senior Technicians and above.
