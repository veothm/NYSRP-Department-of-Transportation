What We Offer Citizens

The Department of Transportation is committed to maintaining safety, reliability, and efficiency across all public roads and highways.

Roadside Assistance

Stranded on Highway 55? Our units provide fuel delivery, tire changes, and minor mechanical repairs to get you back on the road.

Heavy Towing

Locked out or involved in an accident? We offer professional towing and recovery services to any local repair shop or impound lot.

Live Road Updates

Stay informed about active construction in Springfield and the Industrial Park. We manage lane closures to minimize your delay.

Need Assistance?

Our dispatchers are standing by to assist with road hazards or vehicle recovery.
